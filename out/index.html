THE TRADITION BEHIND EKA

Some traditions are too precious to lose.

In Kerala, coconut oil has always been more than an ingredient. It is part of our food, our homes, and our everyday traditions.

Long before modern machines, our grandmothers prepared coconut oil with patience and care. At EKA, we draw inspiration from this heritage through the traditional hot-processing method known as Naadan Urukku Velichennai.

Fresh coconut milk is gently heated in traditional vessels until the oil separates. No complicated story. Just a time-honoured tradition brought into the present.
